## 3. Method

- **Window:** the seven days ending YYYY-MM-DD, inclusive.
- **Beats swept:** releases, research, benchmarks, industry/policy.
- **Verification:** every figure, identifier and date in the report was read from the
  primary source linked above on the accessed date. No claim rests on a search snippet.
- **Left out for lack of verification:** anything that could not be confirmed against a
  primary source, listed here with what was missing.
- **Known gaps:** searches that failed, paywalled sources, beats that were quiet.
